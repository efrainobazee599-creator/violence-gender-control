# Violence, Gender and Control

An interactive exhibit by Efrain Obazee for SOCSCI 1SS3 Engaging Literature, McMaster University.

Research question: How did enslavers use physical and sexual violence to establish and maintain gender-based power and control over enslaved men and women in the American South before the Civil War?
